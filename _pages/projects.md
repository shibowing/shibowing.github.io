## Research Projects

### **Super Odometry**
**Overview:** A robust state estimation system designed for challenging environments where traditional SLAM systems fail.

**Key Features:**
- IMU-centric sensor fusion architecture
- Robust to extreme lighting and weather conditions
- Real-time performance on embedded systems
- Successfully deployed in DARPA Subterranean Challenge

**Technologies:** C++, ROS, CUDA, OpenCV, PCL

**Impact:** Adopted by Team Explorer for autonomous navigation in extreme environments

### **SubT-MRS Dataset**
**Overview:** A comprehensive dataset for pushing SLAM towards all-weather environments.

**Key Features:**
- Multi-modal sensor data (LiDAR, camera, IMU)
- Extreme weather conditions
- Ground truth annotations
- Open-source availability

**Impact:** Enabling research in weather-robust SLAM systems

### **Thermal-Inertial Odometry (TP-TIO)**
**Overview:** A robust thermal-inertial odometry system with deep thermal point detection.

**Key Features:**
- Thermal-visual sensor fusion
- Deep learning for thermal feature detection
- Robust to low-visibility conditions

**Impact:** Enabling autonomous navigation in adverse weather and lighting conditions
