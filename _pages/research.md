## Research Vision

My long-term research goal is to enable autonomous systems to operate reliably in extreme and unstructured environments through robust perception, localization, and mapping capabilities. The central theme of my research is developing **robust SLAM systems** that can handle challenging conditions where traditional approaches fail.

## Research Areas

### **1. Robust SLAM for Challenging Environments**
How can we develop SLAM systems that work reliably in extreme conditions where traditional approaches fail?

**Key Work:**
- **Super Odometry**: IMU-centric sensor fusion for challenging environments
- **Thermal-Inertial Odometry**: Multi-modal fusion for adverse conditions  
- **All-weather SLAM**: Dataset and methods for weather-robust localization

### **2. Multi-Modal Sensor Fusion**
How can we effectively combine different sensor modalities for robust perception in challenging environments?

**Key Work:**
- **LiDAR-Visual-Inertial Fusion**: Real-time state estimation in extreme conditions
- **Thermal-Visual Fusion**: Robust perception in low-visibility and adverse weather
- **Multi-sensor Calibration**: Automatic calibration for complex sensor suites

### **3. 3D Reconstruction in Adverse Conditions**
How can we reconstruct 3D environments when traditional methods fail due to challenging conditions?

**Key Work:**
- **Challenging Environment Reconstruction**: Methods for extreme weather and lighting
- **Real-time 3D Mapping**: Efficient algorithms for embedded systems
- **Robust Feature Extraction**: Reliable features in adverse conditions

## Research Impact

- **DARPA Subterranean Challenge**: Successfully deployed Super Odometry in extreme underground environments
- **Industry Applications**: Technology adopted by multiple robotics companies for challenging environment navigation
- **Open Source**: Released datasets and code for community use and research advancement
- **Academic Recognition**: Papers at top-tier conferences (CVPR, IROS, ICRA) with significant citations
