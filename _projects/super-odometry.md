**Overview:** Super Odometry is a robust state estimation system designed for challenging environments where traditional SLAM systems fail.

**Key Features:**
- IMU-centric sensor fusion architecture
- Robust to extreme lighting and weather conditions
- Real-time performance on embedded systems
- Successfully deployed in DARPA Subterranean Challenge

**Technologies:** C++, ROS, CUDA, OpenCV, PCL

**Impact:** Adopted by Team Explorer for autonomous navigation in extreme environments
